F-Locker for Windows (portable)
================================

Nothing to install. Put this whole folder on a USB stick or anywhere you like,
then double-click F-Locker.exe.

- Your locked files are kept in the "FLockerData" folder next to F-Locker.exe.
  Keep that folder with the app; copy both to move your locker.
- Everything inside FLockerData is AES-256 encrypted.
- Keep your 10-letter recovery key somewhere safe. Without the password and
  the key, nobody can open the locked files - not even you.
